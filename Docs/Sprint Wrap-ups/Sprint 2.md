## Reflection 
- This was a lot longer and wider sprint compared to sprint 1. I took alot longer due to trips, work and outside fun with friends, but it was also a larger learning model. I not only had to learn how to connect views but to also move data between them. I mean got recipe to open New Recipe and then open an ingredient editor view and then tie all those back in order to save the data sequentially in the previous views. I also learned alot about the keyboard after many failed attempts at dismissing them. Taking the prototype out of the canvas is IMPORTANT. It is the most human use test we have. the canvas runs it from a computer base but when used in an actual app downloaded to the phone I was able to truly find flaws that were crucial to the sprint but also the functionality of the app as a whole.
